Dateline Atlas for Android and iPhone · effective October 15, 2026

The app

Dateline Atlas is free to download and use. The free version shows the top 200 stories with one banner ad. Stories are found through the GDELT Project's open news index; headlines and articles belong to the outlets that published them, and each report links to the original. Reliability and framing scores are made by an AI model. They are judgments, not verdicts, and can be wrong, so check important facts with the original sources.
