Help

Dateline Atlas for Android and iPhone

Get in touch. To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on the app's GitHub page, or use the contact email on the app's store listing. Please say which phone you have and what you were doing.

Free and Upgrade

The free version shows the top 80 stories, a headline search and one banner ad. The Upgrade is a one-time purchase that unlocks every story (300 and more), deep search with filters by country and reliability, and removes the ad. There's no subscription.

Restoring the Upgrade

On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the Sources tab and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Google account (Android) or Apple Account (iPhone) you bought it with. A purchase on Android doesn't carry over to iPhone or the other way round.

Refunds

Purchases go through the app store, so refunds do too: Google Play at Google Play's refund page, Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Weather

Weather is optional and covers the United States only; it comes from the US National Weather Service. Tap the weather button on the globe to turn it on or off. If you said no to location and change your mind, allow location for Dateline Atlas in your phone's settings.

When the news updates

New sweeps land twice a day, after 5:52 AM and 5:52 PM New York time. The app picks them up within 15 minutes, or when you reopen it. Without a connection it shows the last stories it downloaded.

Where the stories come from

Stories are found in the GDELT Project's open index of world news coverage, then grouped, translated and checked by an AI model. The reliability and framing scores are judgments, not verdicts, and can be wrong; every report links to the original articles.
